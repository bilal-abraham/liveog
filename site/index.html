Why liveog

More people tap

A preview that shows what is happening now gets opened, and shared again.

Your brand on every link

Your logo, colours and type, on every link anyone sends.

Drawn in the browser

Covers are drawn by a browser that is already open. No image server to run or pay for.

Private where it counts

Invite codes and share links never show in a preview, a search or a cache.
